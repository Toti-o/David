UnCommonwealth

By David J. Devine

Available Now

Kindle & Paperback

Purchase on Amazon

Grenada. Fifty years of independence. A jubilee celebration. A disavowed spy and scientist returns to the island where he was betrayed. Oisin Finnegan carries secrets that could topple governments. Patricia Arnold, a marine biologist, arrives with her own hidden agenda.

Together, they uncover a deadly conspiracy buried in the spice trade, in the warm Caribbean waters, and in the hearts of those who profit from the island's past. What begins as a personal reckoning becomes a race against time to prevent a catastrophe that could reshape the region.

In UnCommonwealth, David J. Devine weaves a tale of redemption, betrayal, and the enduring power of place. Where the past refuses to stay buried and the future hangs in the balance.
