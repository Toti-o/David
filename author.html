About the Author

David J. Devine

Novelist of suspense and place

📍 Lockport, NY

David Devine is a second-generation Irish-American and American/Grenadian novelist who writes fact-based fiction. A native of San Francisco, he was raised in Marin County and earned a Bachelor of Science in Industrial Engineering & Operations Research from UC Berkeley. He has worked in ocean transportation, management consulting in Silicon Valley, and owned a bicycle store. He is a prolific technical writer who began creative writing with short stories before publishing his debut novel, Hibernia.

His debut novel, Hibernia, is a heist thriller set in Dublin and San Francisco. It explores the true financial loophole of €300 million in unclaimed Irish pounds sitting in a Central Bank vault — a story deeply personal to David, whose father worked for the Hibernia Bank.

His latest novel, UnCommonwealth, is a political thriller set in Grenada during the 50th anniversary of its independence. The story follows Oisin Finnegan, a disavowed spy and scientist, and Patricia Arnold, a marine biologist, as they uncover a deadly conspiracy. The inspiration came from watching the Jubilee celebration with his Grenadian wife.
